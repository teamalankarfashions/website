What is a Kolhapuri Saj?

A Kolhapuri Saj is a Maharashtrian necklace strung with small gold-tone pendants, traditionally 21, each shaped after a symbol of prosperity. It is worn by brides and at every festival that matters, and it is the piece Kolhapur is known for across India.

Our Traditionals collection keeps these forms honest: proportions, bead counts and the dark red thread, finished by hand so the piece sits right on a Nauvari or a Paithani.

Before you order

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes. Every order across India can be paid by Cash on Delivery. You can also pay online with UPI, cards or net banking through Razorpay.

Is this real gold?

No. Alankar makes artificial (imitation) jewellery with a gold-tone or silver-tone finish, designed to look like traditional gold pieces at a fraction of the price.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed within 1–2 days. Delivery in Maharashtra usually takes 2–4 days and 4–7 days for the rest of India.

Can I order a custom or bridal set?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your saree colour, budget and date, and we'll put together a matching set.
